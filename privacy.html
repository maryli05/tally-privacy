Tally Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Tally is a habit tracking app. This policy explains what information Tally handles. The short version: Tally does not collect your personal information.

Information we collect

None. Tally does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend.

Information stored on your device

The habits you create and the days you check them off are saved locally on your device so the app can show them to you. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent anywhere. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Tally does not use analytics tools, advertising, or tracking technologies. It does not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Third parties

We do not share your information with any third party, because we do not collect it. If you make a purchase, Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy, and we do not receive your payment details.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Tally schedules notifications locally on your device. No notification content or schedule is sent to us.

Children's privacy

Tally does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
